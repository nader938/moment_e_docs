Using moment·e

A guide to navigating the app and completing your study tasks.

Signing In

When you first open moment·e, you'll see the sign-in screen. You have two options:

  • Username & Password — use the credentials provided by your study coordinator
  • Sign in with Google — if your study uses Google-based accounts
Don't have credentials? Contact your study coordinator. Accounts are created by the research team — you cannot self-register.

Home Screen

After signing in you'll see the Home screen, which shows:

  • Pending tasks — surveys or assessments waiting to be completed
  • Today's schedule — your EMA prompt windows for the day
  • Quick access — buttons for common tasks

Completing Tasks

Tasks include surveys, cognitive assessments, and check-ins. When you tap a task:

1

Read the instructions

Each task opens with a brief description. Read it carefully before starting.

2

Complete at your own pace

Answer honestly. Some tasks are timed — a progress indicator will show if so.

3

Submit

Tap Submit when done. Your responses are automatically encrypted and uploaded.

Need internet to submit Tasks can be completed offline, but an internet connection is required to upload your responses. The app will sync automatically when you reconnect.

Notifications

moment·e sends push notifications when it's time to complete an EMA prompt. Make sure notifications are enabled in your device settings.

  • iOS: Settings → moment·e → Notifications → Allow
  • Android: Settings → Apps → moment·e → Notifications → Enable

Profile

Tap the profile icon to view your account details, study information, and sign out.

Checking Your Version

Your app version is displayed on the splash screen when the app opens, shown as vXX.XX next to the moment·e logo. The current version is v13.04.

Having trouble? Submit a bug report — include your platform (iOS/Android), version number, and what happened.
