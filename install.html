Android Guide

iOS Installation

1

Open the App Store

On your iPhone or iPad, tap the App Store icon.

2

Search for moment·e

Tap the Search tab and type moment-e or ask your study coordinator for a direct download link.

3

Tap Get

Tap Get then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password.

4

Open and sign in

Once installed, open moment·e and sign in with the credentials provided by your study coordinator.

iOS version moment·e requires iOS 13.0 or later. Compatible with iPhone and iPad.

Android Installation

1

Open Google Play

On your Android device, open the Google Play Store app.

2

Search for moment·e

Search for moment-e or ask your study coordinator for a direct link.

3

Tap Install

Tap Install and wait for the download to complete.

4

Open and sign in

Open moment·e and sign in with your study credentials.

Android version Requires Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later, API level 26+.

Requirements

  • iOS: iPhone or iPad running iOS 13.0 or later
  • Android: Device running Android 8.0 (API 26) or later
  • Internet: Required for sign-in and data sync
  • Storage: ~100 MB free space
  • Account: Credentials provided by your study coordinator

Troubleshooting

Can't find the app? Contact your study coordinator for a direct install link, or submit an issue and we'll help.
