# About moment·e

A clinical research platform for ecological momentary assessment, developed at SDSU.

moment·e is a mobile research application for iOS and Android built to support ecological momentary assessment (EMA) studies. It captures real-time data on mental health, behavior, and physiology — wherever participants are in their daily lives.

The app replaces a legacy Delphi-based platform and was rebuilt from the ground up in Flutter to support modern iOS and Android devices with a unified codebase.

## Research Context

moment·e was developed by the **CUTA Lab at San Diego State University** to support longitudinal mental health research. It is designed to study:

- Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)
- Social Phobia (SP)
- Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)
- Related behavioral and physiological patterns

:::info For study participants
If you are taking part in a research study, your study coordinator will provide login credentials and explain what data is collected.
:::
