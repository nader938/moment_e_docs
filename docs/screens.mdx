### Quick Access buttons

Tap any tile to go directly to that module. The welcome card shows live BPM while the wrist sensor is active.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🧠 **Fearless** | Opens the exposure / hierarchy module for your study group. |
| 📈 **My Graph** | View personal data graphs - survey scores, sensor readings, task performance over time. |
| 📊 **Cognitive Tasks** | Launch any of the four cognitive assessments. |
| 📋 **Questionnaires** | Complete surveys assigned to your study group. |
| 💓 **Sensor Data** | View live and historical HRV, accelerometer, GPS, and heart-rate readings. |
| 🤖 **AI Agent** | Coming soon - AI assistant for study-related questions. |

### The four cognitive tasks

| Task | Section | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| ❤️ **Heartbeat Counting** | Interoception | Interoceptive accuracy, metacognitive confidence |
| ⬅️ **Flanker Task** | Reaction Time | Response inhibition, selective attention speed |
| 🔤 **Scrambled Sentences** | Cognitive Bias | Interpretation bias toward OCD-relevant themes |
| 🔗 **Word-Sentence Association** | Cognitive Bias | Bias index, CBM-I assessment & training |

Each task is timed. Results are encrypted and synced automatically after submission.

## Questionnaire Screen

Survey questions appear one at a time. Tap your answer on the Likert scale list, then tap **Next**.

<div className="screen-cap">

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **0–4 scale rows** | Tap a row to select your answer. Options range from *Not at all* to *Extremely*. |
| **Next →** | Advances to the next question. Appears after you select an answer. |
| **Progress bar** | Thin bar at the top shows how far through the questionnaire you are. |

:::tip Offline support
You can answer questions without internet. Responses are stored locally and uploaded automatically when you reconnect.
:::

## Scrambled Sentence Task

Words are shown in a scrambled list. Tap them in the correct order to form a grammatical sentence. A countdown timer runs while you work.

<div className="phone-wrap reverse">
<div className="phone-frame">
<div className="phone-screen">
  <img src="/moment_e_docs/img/screen_sst_task.png" alt="Scrambled Sentence Task showing word list and timer" />
</div>
</div>
<div className="screen-cap">

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Word list** | Tap words one by one in the order they should appear in the sentence. |
| **Time left: XX sec** | Countdown timer. Work quickly - the task is timed. |
| **Unselect** | Clears your current selection so you can start over. |
| **Last** | Goes back to the previous sentence. |
| **Submit** | Submits your completed sentence and moves to the next item. |

</div>
</div>

## Profile & Settings

Tap the **☰** menu icon in the app bar to access account details, study group info, and sign-out.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| 👤 **Account details** | View your username, email, and account creation date. |
| 📊 **Study group** | Shows your current study group and participant level. |
| 🔔 **Notifications** | Check your EMA schedule and notification preferences. |
| ↩ **Sign out** | Signs you out. Locally cached data is preserved until the next sync. |

:::note Version number
The app version is shown at the bottom of the profile screen as `v13.04`. Include this when reporting a bug.
:::
