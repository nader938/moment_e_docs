# About moment·e

A clinical research platform for ecological momentary assessment, developed at SDSU.

moment·e is a mobile research application for iOS and Android built to support EMA studies. It captures real-time data on mental health, behavior, and physiology — wherever participants are in their daily lives. The app replaces a legacy Delphi-based platform, rebuilt from the ground up in Flutter.

:::info For study participants
Your study coordinator will provide login credentials and explain what data is collected.
:::
