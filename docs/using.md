# Using moment·e

A guide to navigating the app and completing your study tasks.

## Signing In

When you first open moment·e, you'll see the sign-in screen. You have two options:

- **Username & Password** — use the credentials provided by your study coordinator
- **Sign in with Google** — if your study uses Google-based accounts

:::note Don't have credentials?
Contact your study coordinator. Accounts are created by the research team — you cannot self-register.
:::

## Home Screen

After signing in you'll see the Home screen, which shows:

- **Welcome card** — your username and live heart-rate sensor reading (BPM)
- **Quick Access grid** — six buttons for every major module
- **Today's schedule** — your EMA prompt windows for the day

See [App Screens](./screens) for a full visual guide to every button.

## Completing Tasks

Tasks include surveys, cognitive assessments, and check-ins. When you tap a task:

1. **Read the instructions** — Each task opens with a brief description. Read it carefully before starting.
2. **Complete at your own pace** — Answer honestly. Some tasks are timed — a progress indicator will show if so.
3. **Submit** — Tap **Submit** when done. Your responses are automatically encrypted and uploaded.

:::warning Internet required to submit
Tasks can be completed offline, but an internet connection is required to upload your responses. The app will sync automatically when you reconnect.
:::

## Notifications

moment·e sends push notifications when it's time to complete an EMA prompt. Make sure notifications are enabled in your device settings.

- **iOS:** Settings → moment·e → Notifications → Allow
- **Android:** Settings → Apps → moment·e → Notifications → Enable

## Profile

Tap the menu icon (☰) in the app bar to view your account details, study information, and sign out.

## Checking Your Version

Your app version is displayed on the **splash screen** when the app opens, shown as `vXX.XX` next to the moment·e logo. The current version is **v13.04**.

:::tip Having trouble?
[Submit a bug report](https://github.com/nader938/moment_e_issues/issues/new) — include your platform (iOS/Android), version number, and what happened.
:::
