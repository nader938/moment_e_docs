Distress Tolerance

CEQI Domain #5

Tolerating Discomfort

Feeling uncomfortable during exposures is not a sign something is wrong — it is exactly where change happens. This module helps participants understand, reframe, and lean into that discomfort.

~20 minutes
5 topics
Interactive + reflection
What's covered
1
Discomfort ≠ Danger

Reframe fight-or-flight symptoms as uncomfortable but not catastrophic.

4 min
2
Why We Choose Discomfort

Connect exposure to everyday choices people already make for long-term gain.

4 min
3
Find Your Own Value

Recall a time discomfort led to something worthwhile.

5 min
4
A Good Sign

Discomfort during exposure means change is actively happening.

4 min
5
Apply to Your Exposures

Reflection prompt tied to the participant's own hierarchy.

3 min
Live module

This is the exact experience participants see in the app. Tap through it just as they would.

Clinical notes
🧠
Inhibitory learning

Discomfort is reframed as evidence that new associative learning is occurring — not a signal to escape. This aligns with inhibitory learning models of exposure therapy.

💪
Values-based framing

Participants connect exposure to things they already voluntarily tolerate discomfort for: dentist visits, workouts, job interviews. This reduces the uniqueness of exposure difficulty.

✍️
Personalised reflection

The final screen prompts participants to write what they stand to gain from a specific exposure on their own hierarchy, anchoring the module to individual treatment goals.

🔀
Pacing choice

Participants choose between step-by-step and free exploration at the start, supporting autonomy and accommodating different learning preferences.

Note for clinicians: This module is designed to be completed independently by participants between sessions. Review the participant's reflection from Screen 5 in the following session to reinforce the values-discomfort link and connect it to upcoming exposure practice.
