A visual walkthrough of every screen in moment·e v13.04 — what each button does and when to use it.

Quick Access buttons

Tap any tile to go directly to that module. The welcome card at the top shows your live BPM from the wrist sensor while it's active.

  • Fearless Opens your study's exposure or hierarchy module (or a direct link if your study group has a URL configured).
  • My Graph View your personal data graphs — survey scores, sensor readings, and task performance over time.
  • Cognitive Tasks Launch any of the four cognitive assessments: Heartbeat Counting, Flanker, Scrambled Sentences, or Word-Sentence Association.
  • Questionnaires Complete scheduled or ad-hoc survey questionnaires assigned to your study group.
  • Sensor Data View live and historical sensor readings: HRV, accelerometer, GPS, and heart rate from the wrist sensor.
  • AI Agent Coming soon — an AI assistant for participants to ask study-related questions.

The four cognitive tasks

Each task targets a different cognitive domain. Your results are encrypted and synced to the study server automatically after submission.

  • ❤️ Heartbeat Counting Count your heartbeats silently across 6 timed intervals without touching your pulse. Measures interoceptive accuracy and metacognitive confidence. Section: Interoception.
  • ⬅️ Flanker Task Tap the direction the center arrow points, ignoring the surrounding arrows. Measures response inhibition and selective attention speed. Section: Reaction Time.
  • 🔤 Scrambled Sentences Tap words in the correct order to form a grammatical sentence from a scrambled list. Assesses interpretation bias toward OCD-relevant themes. Section: Cognitive Bias.
  • 🔗 Word-Sentence Association Decide whether a word matches a sentence's meaning. Measures and retrains interpretation bias using CBM-I. Section: Cognitive Bias.

Questionnaire Screen

Survey questions appear one at a time on a clean screen. Each question shows a Likert-scale list of response options — tap your answer and continue.

Answering a question

Questions are displayed one at a time with numbered response options. Tap a row to select your answer, then tap Next to continue.

  • 0–4 Scale Most questions use a 5-point scale from Not at all to Extremely. The exact labels depend on the question type.
  • Next → Advances to the next question. Appears in the lower-right corner once you've made a selection.
  • Progress bar A thin bar at the top shows how far through the questionnaire you are.
💡 Offline support
You can answer questions without internet. Responses are stored locally and uploaded automatically when you reconnect.

Scrambled Sentence Task

The Scrambled Sentence Task (SST) presents a list of words. Tap them in the correct order to form a grammatical sentence. A countdown timer runs in the lower portion of the screen.

Scrambled Sentence Task controls

Words are shown as tappable items. Tap them one by one in the right order. Your assembled sentence appears below the word list in real time.

  • Word list Tap words in the order they should appear in the sentence. Each tapped word moves to the sentence preview below.
  • Time left: XX sec A countdown timer shows how many seconds remain. The task is timed — work quickly but accurately.
  • Unselect Clears your current word selection so you can start the sentence over.
  • Last Goes back to the previous sentence if you need to review it.
  • Submit Submits your completed sentence and moves to the next item. Only appears once a sentence is assembled.

Profile menu

Access your account details and study configuration from the profile screen.

  • 👤 Account details View your username, email, and account creation date.
  • 📊 Study group Shows your current study group and participant level. Contact your coordinator to change this.
  • 🔔 Notifications Check your EMA schedule and notification preferences. Ensure notifications are enabled in device settings.
  • ↩ Sign out Signs you out of the app. Your locally cached data is preserved until the next sync.
📋 Version number
The app version is shown at the bottom of the profile screen as v13.04. Include this when reporting a bug.
