App Screens

A visual guide to every screen in moment·e v13.04 — what each button does and when to use it.

Quick Access buttons​

Tap any tile to go directly to that module. The welcome card shows live BPM while the wrist sensor is active.

ButtonWhat it does
🧠 FearlessOpens the exposure / hierarchy module for your study group.
📈 My GraphView personal data graphs — survey scores, sensor readings, task performance over time.
📊 Cognitive TasksLaunch any of the four cognitive assessments.
📋 QuestionnairesComplete surveys assigned to your study group.
💓 Sensor DataView live and historical HRV, accelerometer, GPS, and heart-rate readings.
🤖 AI AgentComing soon — AI assistant for study-related questions.

Each task is timed. Results are encrypted and synced automatically after submission.

Questionnaire Screen​

Survey questions appear one at a time. Tap your answer on the Likert scale list, then tap Next.

ButtonWhat it does
0–4 scale rowsTap a row to select your answer. Options range from Not at all to Extremely.
Next →Advances to the next question. Appears after you select an answer.
Progress barThin bar at the top shows how far through the questionnaire you are.
Offline support

You can answer questions without internet. Responses are stored locally and uploaded automatically when you reconnect.

Scrambled Sentence Task​

Words are shown in a scrambled list. Tap them in the correct order to form a grammatical sentence. A countdown timer runs while you work.

ButtonWhat it does
Word listTap words one by one in the order they should appear in the sentence.
Time left: XX secCountdown timer. Work quickly — the task is timed.
UnselectClears your current selection so you can start over.
LastGoes back to the previous sentence.
SubmitSubmits your completed sentence and moves to the next item.

Profile & Settings​

Tap the ☰ menu icon in the app bar to access account details, study group info, and sign-out.

OptionWhat it does
👤 Account detailsView your username, email, and account creation date.
📊 Study groupShows your current study group and participant level.
🔔 NotificationsCheck your EMA schedule and notification preferences.
↩ Sign outSigns you out. Locally cached data is preserved until the next sync.
Version number

The app version is shown at the bottom of the profile screen as v13.04. Include this when reporting a bug.
