iOS & Android

Native apps on both platforms. Works on iPhone, iPad, and Android devices running recent OS versions.

Encrypted & Secure

All survey responses, sensor data, and task results are AES-256 encrypted before upload to secure cloud storage.

Cognitive Tasks

Includes validated cognitive assessments: Flanker, CEQI, Stop Signal, Scrambled Sentences, and more.

📊

Real-Time Data

Sensor readings, HRV, accelerometer, and GPS captured continuously and synced to S3 for analysis.

⏰

EMA Scheduling

Configurable daily prompt schedules push notifications at the right moments throughout the day.

🏥

Clinical Research

Designed for OCD, GAD, Social Phobia, and related mental health research studies at SDSU CUTA Lab.
